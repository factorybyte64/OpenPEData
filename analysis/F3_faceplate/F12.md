Together with F05, F16, F40 this yields a common enumeration of the HMI data types: 1 Bool, 3 Int, 5 LInt, 9 ULInt, 10 Real, 11 LReal, 13 DateTime, 14 WString, 30 Struct, bit 0x40 = Array; 0x502 Color, 0x100xx reference types (properties only).

**Hidden device.** Version 0.0.6 has 102 objects, 11 more than 0.0.5: the 5 new HmiTagData in the interface tag table, 4 `HmiArrayMemberTagData` (elements T_Array[0..3]) and 2 `HmiStructureMemberTagData` (elements of T_Udt). Array and structure elements now count as content-dependent (CONTENT_TYPES); the fixed core stays 79.
